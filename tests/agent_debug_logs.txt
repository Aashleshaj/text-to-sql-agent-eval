QUESTION: What is the maximum total GVA (in millions) for boroughs that have a disruption rate of 100% in the BoroughDailyTrend table and a 'High' risk band in the BoroughRisk table?
RAW AGENT RESPONSE: The maximum total GVA is **£24,637.5 million**.

This belongs to **Lambeth**, which has:
- Disruption rate: 100%
- Risk band: High
- Total GVA: £24,637.5 million

The result appears 3 times in the BoroughDailyTrend table, likely due to multiple daily records for the same borough.
EXTRACTED SQL: 'SELECT MAX(bdt.total_gva_m) as max_total_gva
FROM BoroughDailyTrend bdt
JOIN BoroughRisk br ON bdt.borough = br.borough
WHERE bdt.disruption_rate_pct = 100.0
AND br.risk_band = 'High''

QUESTION: Which boroughs have a 'High' risk band in the *BoroughRisk* table and a total GVA greater than 5000 million in the *BoroughSummary* table?
RAW AGENT RESPONSE: **Results:**

Two boroughs meet both criteria:

| Borough | Risk Band | Total GVA (£m) |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Lambeth | High | 24,637.5 |
| Brent | High | 17,411.3 |

Both boroughs have a **High** risk band in the *BoroughRisk* table and a total GVA greater than 5,000 million in the *BoroughSummary* table.
EXTRACTED SQL: 'SELECT 
    r.borough,
    r.risk_band,
    s.total_gva_m
FROM BoroughRisk r
JOIN BoroughSummary s ON r.borough = s.borough
WHERE r.risk_band = 'High'
  AND s.total_gva_m > 5000
ORDER BY s.total_gva_m DESC;'
